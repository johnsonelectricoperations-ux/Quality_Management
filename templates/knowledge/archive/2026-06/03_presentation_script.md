# 발표 대본 (참고용 — 답변 톤을 이 대본과 일치시킬 것)

## Slide 1 — Cost of Poor Quality
"This slide shows our Cost of Poor Quality.
In June, our actual COPQ was 0.35%. Our target was 0.31%. But year-to-date, we are still within target at 0.27%.
We had 40 million won in scrap cost. 17 million won came from a reformer failure. This affected gas supply to our TM part sintering process. This was the main reason for the increase from last month.
We also had 9 million won in customer cost. This was related to a damping force issue at Mando. Our third action item addresses this — improved tooling design.
We already repaired the generator and restored gas supply. We also updated our PM checklist to prevent this happening again."

## Slide 2 — Total Internal Scrap Cost
"This slide shows internal scrap cost.
In June, this was 0.30%. Our target was 0.17%. Again, the reformer failure was the main reason.
By team: Team 2, transmission parts, was 70% of the cost. Team 1, VMS parts, was 30%.
The reason is simple. Transmission parts cost more per piece. So even a small scrap quantity creates a big cost impact. This month, the reformer issue made this effect bigger.
We will continue process improvement to reduce this cost."

## Slide 3 — Scrap Qty as % of Total Produced
"This slide shows scrap quantity, not cost.
In June, this was 0.15%. Our target was 0.17%. So we achieved this target.
By team: Team 1, VMS parts, was 91% of the quantity. Team 2, transmission parts, was only 9%.
This looks opposite from the cost slide. But it's not a problem. It's simply because transmission parts cost more per piece. A small quantity still creates a large cost.
Going forward, we will continue preventive process checks and analyze damage factors between processes."
